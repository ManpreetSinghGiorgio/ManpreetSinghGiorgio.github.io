I am a graduate student in **Economics & Data Analysis** at the **University of Verona**, completing an **MSc in Finance** and holding a **BA (Hons) in Management & Economics** from the Open University Business School.

My work bridges macroeconomic policy, financial markets, and real-world business dynamics—analyzed through quantitative data methods.

Targeting institutional research and policy surveillance (ECB, Banca d'Italia, European Commission). I focus on how monetary policy, global macro trends, and geopolitical shifts transmit through financial markets and corporate balance sheets. Driven by disciplined economic theory, empirical data, and practical intuition—mindful that in policy and data, *"there are no facts, only interpretations."*

---

## Research & Focus Areas

* **Macroeconomic Surveillance & Monetary Policy:** Central bank reaction functions, monetary transmission channels, and Euro-area financial stability.
* **Financial Markets & Balance Sheet Dynamics:** Sovereign yield spreads (BTP-Bund), corporate credit risk, and institutional liquidity resilience.
* **Geo-Economics & Global Trade:** Economic statecraft, supply chain reconfiguration, and the impact of geopolitical fragmentation on macro fundamentals.
* **Firm Behavior & Real Economy Transmission:** How macro and financial shocks affect pricing power, investment decisions, and corporate solvency.
* **Quantitative & Data Tooling:** Exploratory data analysis, time-series tracking, optimization modeling, and reproducible empirical pipelines.
