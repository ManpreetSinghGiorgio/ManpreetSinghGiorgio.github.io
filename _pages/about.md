# Manpreet Singh
### Applied Macroeconomics • Financial Stability • Geo-Economic Risk

> **"There are no facts, only interpretations."** — *F. Nietzsche*  
> Grounding economic statecraft and policy analysis in empirical data, structural models, and institutional reality.

I am an economics graduate student at the **University of Verona** (*MSc Economics & Data Analysis*) and finishing an **MSc in Finance** through the **Open University Business School**. 

My focus centers on the transmission mechanisms linking **central bank decisions, financial market fragility, and real-economy corporate balance sheets**. By pairing formal macroeconomic theory with quantitative data pipelines, I investigate how geopolitical shifts and monetary tightening impact European financial stability.
